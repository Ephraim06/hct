essential FAQs

about the places we visit, not the VR stuff

Do we actually go up Table Mountain?
Absolutely. Our Table Mountain & Cape of Good Hope tour includes cable car ride (weather permitting) or hiking option. You'll have free time at the summit.
Are there penguins all year at Boulders?
Yes, the African penguin colony is resident year‑round. Boulders Beach is part of every penguin tour, and you'll see them up close from boardwalks.
Which game reserve for the Safari tour?
We use a private Big Five reserve (Aquila or Inverdoorn depending on season). Both offer luxury vehicles, guides, and buffet lunch included.
When is whale season in Hermanus?
Peak whale watching is from July to November. Southern right whales come close to shore; our full‑day tour includes boat or land-based viewing.
