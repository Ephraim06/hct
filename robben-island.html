Robben Island &
Township Cultural Tour

Explore Robben Island and experience authentic township culture on this full-day guided tour

R2750 / person 9 hours

Overview

Experience South Africa's powerful history and vibrant culture on this full-day Robben Island and Township Tour. The journey begins with a visit to Robben Island, a UNESCO World Heritage Site best known as the prison where Nelson Mandela was held for 18 years.

After returning to the mainland, the tour continues with a guided township experience, offering meaningful insight into local communities, daily life, and South Africa's journey beyond apartheid.

Tour highlights

  • Visit the iconic Robben Island, a UNESCO World Heritage Site
  • Scenic ferry crossing from the V&A Waterfront
  • Explore historic and vibrant townships with a knowledgeable local guide
  • Learn about South Africa's past, present, and community life
  • Small-group, respectful, and educational experience

Tour Itinerary

08:00
Cape Town – Pick up

Morning pick-up from selected Cape Town areas, including the CBD and Atlantic Seaboard. Your guide will provide an overview of the day before heading to the V&A Waterfront.

09:00
Robben Island Tour

Board the ferry to Robben Island and enjoy a guided experience that includes:

  • Visit to the maximum-security prison
  • Insightful storytelling about South Africa's political history
13:00
Estimated Lunch break

Free time for lunch at the V&A Waterfront or en route (own account).

14:30
Township Cultural Tour

In the afternoon, enjoy a guided township tour in Langa – Cape Town's oldest formal township. The experience focuses on respectful interaction, storytelling, and understanding local life.

17:00
Drop-off

Return to your accommodation in the late afternoon.
