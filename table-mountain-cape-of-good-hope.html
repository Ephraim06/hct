TABLE MOUNTAIN · CAPE PENINSULA · PENGUINS

Table Mountain
& Cape of Good Hope

Includes Table Mountain, Chapman's Peak Drive, Cape of Good Hope and Boulders Beach penguins

R1150 / person 9.5 hours

Overview

Embark on an unforgettable journey through Cape Town's most iconic landmarks. From the breathtaking heights of Table Mountain to the scenic beauty of Chapman's Peak Drive, this day tour offers a perfect blend of adventure and relaxation. Discover the rugged charm of the Cape of Good Hope, and get up close with the adorable penguins at Boulders Beach.

Tour highlights

  • Ride the Table Mountain Aerial Cableway for panoramic views of Cape Town
  • Stop at Hout Bay harbour
  • Drive along the world-famous Chapman's Peak Drive
  • Discover the Cape of Good Hope Nature Reserve and capture the iconic sign photo
  • Visit Cape Point Lighthouse where the two oceans currents meet
  • Stop at Boulders Beach to see the African penguin colony
  • Enjoy stunning coastal and mountain scenery throughout the journey

Tour Itinerary

07:30
Cape Town – Pick up

Pick up from your accommodation in Cape Town.

08:30
Table Mountain

Towering above Cape Town, Table Mountain gives you amazing views of the city and the ocean. The flat top is a great spot to enjoy the beautiful scenery.

10:30
Hout Bay Harbour

This charming fishing village is located at the base of the mountains, with a bustling harbor and a lively market.

11:15
Chapman's Peak Drive

This scenic road winds along the rugged coastline, offering breathtaking views of the ocean and mountains. It's a great spot for a leisurely drive, or for hiking or cycling.

12:00
Cape of Good Hope Nature Reserve

This stunning nature reserve is located at the southernmost tip of the Cape Peninsula. The reserve is home to an array of wildlife, including baboons, ostriches, and over 250 species of birds. Visitors can hike up to the lighthouse for panoramic views of the coastline, or take the funicular up to the top.

13:30
Lunch

Flexible lunch stop (own expense).

14:30
Boulders Beach

This sheltered cove is home to a colony of endangered African penguins, which visitors can view up close on the beach. The beach itself is also a great spot for swimming and sunbathing.

17:00
Drop-off at your accommodation

Tour ends with drop-off at your accommodation.
