SINCE 2018

We turn ordinary holidays into extraordinary memories

"Adventure with a smile – that's our promise."

Happy Cape Tours was born from a simple idea: to share the raw beauty of the Western Cape with genuine warmth and expertise. What started as two friends showing travellers their favourite hidden spots has grown into a boutique tour company known for personal touches and unforgettable days.

We specialise in creating stress-free experiences that showcase the very best of the Mother City — from the iconic Table Mountain and breathtaking Cape Point, to hidden beaches, vibrant townships, and world-class wine routes.

Whether you're visiting for the first time or returning for more, our small-group and private tours are designed with one goal in mind: to make every moment fun, safe, and full of smiles.
