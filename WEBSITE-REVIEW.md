## Priority 1 — lost enquiries and blocked booking paths

1. **Contact form falsely reports successful delivery.** `contact.html:790–815` simulates a request with a timer, saves data in localStorage and resets the form. No request delivers the enquiry to the business. The success modal at line 555 promises a reply. Connect a real submission service, validate on the server and show success only after confirmed acceptance; retain entered data on failure.
2. **All eight availability buttons are inert.** `cape-point-penguins.html:646`, `cape-point-penguins-private-tour.html:646`, `cape-agulhas.html:529`, `chapmans-peak.html:632`, `capewinelands-stellenbosch.html:348`, `robben-island.html:552`, `safari-day-tour.html:585`, `whale-watching.html:564`. They have no link or handler. Route to a working enquiry/booking flow with the selected tour prefilled.
3. **Phone and WhatsApp destinations disagree with the displayed number.** Main phone links use `+2723078502` instead of the international equivalent of displayed `078 230 8502`: `+27782308502` (e.g. `index.html:333`, `contact.html:274,328` and most tour headers). `about.html:247` uses `wa.me/2782308502`; `contact.html:501,522` uses `wa.me/2723078502`. Standardize to the verified business number across every contact channel. Existing footer/floating links generally already use `27782308502`.
4. **Broken internal tour links.** Footer links repeatedly target nonexistent `robben-island-township.html` and `whale-watching-hermanus.html`; existing files are `robben-island.html` and `whale-watching.html`. Agulhas links in `cape-agulhas.html:712` and `capewinelands-stellenbosch.html:414,469` target nonexistent `cape-agulhas-full-day-tour.html`; use `cape-agulhas.html`. Add redirects too if the old URLs have previously been public.

## Priority 2 — incorrect content and accessibility

5. **Tour filtering misclassifies penguin tours.** `tours.html:348,392` assigns both Cape of Good Hope/penguin cards to `wine`. They disappear from the nature filter and appear under wine. Correct categories; consider multiple category values for tours with several themes.
6. **Copied wine text on penguin tour cards.** `tours.html:372,417` describes the tours as “Franschhoek · tastings”. The private card image at line 394 also has `alt="Winelands"`. Replace with matching coastal tour details and descriptive alternative text.
7. **Placeholder navigation.** All pages contain social links with `href="#"`; additional empty links occur in tour/footer navigation (e.g. `contact.html:674`). Set real destinations or remove inactive links. A page jump or self-navigation is not a useful result.
8. **Mobile navigation lacks accessible state and focus management.** Shared menu scripts only toggle a CSS class (e.g. `index.html:1018–1033`). Add meaningful names to icon-only open/close buttons, `aria-expanded`/`aria-controls`, Escape close, focus restoration and appropriate modal focus handling. The translated-offscreen menu is not explicitly made inert when closed, so its links can remain in keyboard order. Its outside-click test checks the drawer itself, without a separate backdrop dismissal mechanism.
9. **Contact labels are not associated with inputs.** Six labels at `contact.html:395–429` lack `for` attributes and do not wrap their inputs. Associate each with its field ID; add suitable autocomplete attributes and accessible inline error/status feedback.
10. **Gallery requires pointer input to open images.** `.gallery-item` containers are divs with click handlers (`gallery.html:396–450,679–681`); they have no keyboard activation semantics. Lightbox close/previous/next controls are also divs. Use native buttons with accessible names.
11. **Lightbox and form modals lack dialog semantics/focus handling.** `gallery.html:454–480,670–687` and `contact.html:536–563,744–755` do not implement dialog focus capture/restoration. The contact success dialog has no Escape handler. The lightbox image alt remains empty because the scripts update only src/caption. Use accessible dialogs and synchronize image descriptions.
12. **Gallery navigation ignores the selected filter.** `gallery.html:689–703` navigates the complete galleryItems array, including hidden categories. Navigate the currently filtered collection so the next/previous buttons respect the user's selection.
13. **Gallery image metadata is inconsistent.** `gallery.html:415` labels `images/cape-point.jpg` as Chapman’s Peak; the penguin tile at line 439 is categorized as landscape, and captions are maintained in a separate positional array (`:653–668`). Confirm the intended image, correct category/description and derive captions from each item to avoid mismatches.
14. **Nested interactive elements.** Links repeatedly wrap buttons (`index.html:379–388`, `tours.html:377–387` and footer call links). Style anchors directly for navigation; use buttons for actions. This removes invalid interactive nesting and confusing keyboard semantics.
15. **No reduced-motion accommodation.** Shared styles animate marquees/floating controls continuously and GSAP adds entrance animation. No `prefers-reduced-motion` handling is present. Disable nonessential motion for that preference and provide a keyboard-operable pause for moving content where needed.
16. **Weak page landmarks and heading structure.** All 13 pages have two h1 elements, one for branding and one for page content; no main element is present. Use ordinary branding text, a clear page h1, a main landmark and a skip-to-content link. Multiple h1s alone are not proof of an SEO penalty.

## Priority 3 — loading, reliability and maintainability

17. **Oversized images.** `images/boulders-beach-penguins.jpg` is 11.52 MB and is used in the tours listing. Other examples: hero JPG 2.44 MB, Boulders JPG 2.60 MB, Cape Winelands image 2.17 MB and Cape Point JPG 2.04 MB. Generate appropriately sized WebP/AVIF variants and responsive srcset/sizes. Keep the hero prioritized and lazy-load below-the-fold imagery.
18. **No image loading or dimension hints.** Across 99 img elements, none has lazy loading or both width and height attributes. Some CSS containers already reserve space, so layout shift requires measurement. Supply intrinsic dimensions/aspect ratios and lazy-load suitable offscreen images.
19. **Production CSS is generated by a remote runtime.** Every page loads `https://cdn.tailwindcss.com` in the head. Build a static stylesheet for predictable production delivery, smaller runtime cost and fewer external availability dependencies.
20. **Core interaction initialization depends on animation libraries.** Inline scripts begin with unguarded `gsap.registerPlugin(ScrollTrigger)` before attaching menu, filtering, form and gallery handlers. If GSAP or its plugin fails to load, those handlers are never installed. Initialize core interactions independently and treat animations as optional enhancements.
21. **Repeated page code invites inconsistent fixes.** Headers, footers, CSS and scripts are copied across 13 pages; broken contact/navigation values already differ. Extract shared assets/templates or use a small static-site build. `tours.html:1469–1499` additionally duplicates menu listeners and tour-card animations from its preceding script; consolidate initialization.
22. **Missing pinned-tab icon and duplicate head entries.** All pages reference absent `images/safari-pinned-tab.svg`. Most repeat manifest and mask-icon links (`index.html:33–37`). Supply or remove the icon reference and keep one declaration of each asset.
23. **Manifest is incomplete and points to missing root assets.** `images/site.webmanifest` has empty name/short_name and icon URLs `/android-chrome-192x192.png` and `/android-chrome-512x512.png`; the actual files are under `/images/`. Correct names and paths if retaining installation support.
24. **Unnecessary retention and logging of enquiry data.** `contact.html:794–807` persists names, email, phone and message in localStorage and writes them to the console. Remove this storage/logging from the real delivery flow, and provide a clear privacy notice explaining use of enquiry information. No dedicated privacy or booking terms page is present in the workspace. Business policies should be supplied and confirmed by the operator.

## Content and product improvements requiring business confirmation

- Clearly distinguish shared and private tours, minimum group size, pickup area/time, child pricing, accessibility and mandatory versus optional entry fees. Make total expected cost easy to understand before enquiry.
- Verify published attraction fees, tour prices, seasonal availability, ratings/review counts and external Airbnb/review destinations against authoritative business records. Their current accuracy was not verified in this audit.
- Present cancellation/refund and weather policies consistently across tour pages; some page-specific conditions exist, but there is no central policy page.
- Collect travel date and party size as explicit enquiry fields and preselect the originating tour. The current contact form leaves those details to a free-text message.
- Improve structured data with verified business/contact details and page-appropriate tour/breadcrumb markup. Existing titles, descriptions, canonicals, robots.txt and sitemap.xml are present; they should be preserved and checked against the deployed host.
- Footer copyright text still says 2025; optionally update to 2026 or generate the current year.
