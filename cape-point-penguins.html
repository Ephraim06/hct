Cape of Good Hope
& Penguins

Includes Boulders Beach penguins, Cape Point, Cape of Good Hope and Chapman's Peak Drive.

R1150 / person Small Group 8.5 hours

Overview

Embark on an unforgettable journey to the Cape of Good Hope, one of Cape Town's most iconic landmarks. Our full-day guided tour offers an unparalleled experience, showcasing the breathtaking beauty of the Cape Peninsula. From the dramatic cliffs of Cape Point to the serene beaches of Boulders Beach, home to the famous African penguins, this tour is a must-do for anyone visiting Cape Town.

Tour highlights

  • Scenic drive along the Atlantic Coast via Chapman's Peak
  • Stop at Hout Bay – optional boat trip to Seal Island
  • Explore the breathtaking Cape Point Nature Reserve
  • Visit the famous Cape of Good Hope, the southwestern tip of Africa
  • See the African penguins at Boulders Beach in Simon's Town
  • Experience stunning viewpoints and photo stops along the Cape Peninsula
  • Browse local craft markets and seaside towns
  • Chance to spot wildlife such as baboons, ostriches, and antelope

Tour Itinerary

07:45
Cape Town – Pick up

Pick up from your accommodation.

08:20
Maiden's Cove viewpoint

Enjoy a quick stop at this picturesque viewpoint, offering stunning vistas of the Camps Bay beach, Lion's Head and the Twelve Apostles mountain range.

09:00
Hout Bay harbour

Enjoy a picturesque stop in the quaint fishing village of Hout Bay, with optional boat trips to Seal Island.

09:45
Chapman's Peak Drive

Experience one of the world's most scenic coastal drives, with awe-inspiring views of the Atlantic Ocean.

11:30
Cape Point

Marvel at the stunning views from the Cape Point Lighthouse, offering panoramic vistas of the rugged coastline.

12:00
Cape of Good Hope

Explore the southwestern tip of the Cape Peninsula, known for its rugged beauty and rich history.

13:00
Lunch

Flexible lunch break (own expense).

14:30
Boulders Beach

Visit the charming African penguin colony and enjoy the serene beach setting.

16:30
Tour ends in Cape Town

Drop off at your accommodation.
